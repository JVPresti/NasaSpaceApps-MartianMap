# Credits and NASA sources

Generated by `npm run credits` from the same data as `public/data/credits.json`. Do not edit by hand: change the provenance blocks in `public/data/*.json` or the list in `scripts/credits-sources.mjs`, then run the script.

## What is real, artistic and modelled

- Real: terrain shape and photo (HiRISE DTM 1 m + orthomosaic 25 cm), background (CTX DEM 20 m + mosaic 6 m), position of Cheyava Falls, slope values, sun shadows.
- Artistic: the Mars colour (HiRISE and CTX are grayscale), sky and haze presets, night lamp.
- Modelled or estimated: rock positions (statistical model, not individual real rocks), rock shapes and close-up textures (Earth scans), the Cheyava Falls rock (hand-made after NASA PIA26401), the layered outcrop at Bright Angel (hand-made in the style of the Mastcam-Z images of sol 1217, not a copy of a specific rock), rock-abundance layer.
- Real spot for the "dangerous slope" scene: about 200 m west of Cheyava Falls (x -201, z -52), 34 deg average.
- There is no mesa overhang in this area; elevation data cannot contain overhangs.
- Every number on screen carries a tag: DATA (measured by a mission or stated in a NASA document), DERIVED (calculated by us from DATA) or SIM (our assumption or a game choice). Published observations are historical, not current conditions. RAD measured radiation in Gale crater, not in Jezero.

Source: `docs/marswalk-assets/README.md`.

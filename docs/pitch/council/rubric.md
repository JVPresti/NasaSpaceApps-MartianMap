# Rubric given to every judge

## The project

Marswalk is a NASA Space Apps 2026 entry for the challenge "Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map", by team Los
Frijolitos Espaciales Refritos Nueva Generación (6 people, Ensenada, Mexico). It is a browser app: a mission table
to plan a walk on real Mars terrain in Jezero crater, and a first-person helmet view to walk it, with a "margin"
that shows the oxygen left after walking home and keeping a 30-minute reserve. The local pitch runs inside the
website (a presentation mode) and hands off to a live demo. A 7-slide PDF deck goes to the global judges. A
30-second video goes with the submission.

## The five official criteria (2026 Judging & Awards Guide)

- **Impact**: addresses a minor or major problem; reach from a broad audience to a select few. Top: significant
  problem, extensive reach, inspiring.
- **Creativity**: originality and innovation; a novel concept or a meaningful improvement on existing ideas.
- **Validity**: grounded in scientific principles, technically feasible, usable and user-friendly.
- **Relevance**: fully addresses the challenge and integrates NASA data as a core component, not a superficial
  addition.
- **Presentation**: clearly conveys the challenge, the solution and its significance; engaging, well-structured
  storytelling. Judges evaluate the demo and the full project.

The audience of the deck and the video is "someone who has no context, technical background, or previous knowledge
of your subject".

## Creative rules the team set

- One thesis: "On Mars, the question isn't where you can go. It's where you can go and still get home." Said early,
  said once, then proved.
- Open cold with something concrete, not a greeting.
- Numbers from the app with their source, not adjectives.
- Banned: revolutionary, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, next-generation, immersive, seamless, powerful, innovative,
  leverage, "the final frontier", "one small step", "imagine a world where", "introducing", "the future of
  exploration", "AI-powered", stacked rhetorical questions, "...and beyond", closing on a slogan. Spanish
  equivalents too.
- End on the live demo or one hard fact, not a vision statement.
- Every animation in the presentation mode must teach something. Flag any that only decorates.
- Honesty: what is measured versus simulated must be clear.

## Severity

- **blocker**: a judge would misunderstand the problem or the product, a claim is false, or it runs over time.
- **major**: a Space Apps judge would notice in the first minute and mark it down, or a line could appear unchanged
  in another team's pitch.
- **minor**: polish.

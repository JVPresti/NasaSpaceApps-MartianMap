The English version of `script-5min-es.md`, line for line, for rehearsal with non-Spanish speakers and for the global round. Same beats (`beats.md`), same demo (`demo-run-sheet.md`, cut A), same numbers.

**Thesis:** on Mars, the question isn't where you can go. It's where you can go and still get home. Said once, early. Everything after proves it.

# 8-minute version script (English)

The English version of `script-8min-es.md`, line for line, for rehearsal. Same demo (`docs/DEMO.md`, segments 1 to 11), same numbers.

> **Confirm with the Ensenada organisers:** `docs/DEMO.md` (#282) points to an 8 minute demo, and we assumed a 5 minute pitch. This script covers the 8 minute case, with the full DEMO.md demo, 35 seconds of story before it and 50 after.

The demo is all of `docs/DEMO.md`, segments 1 to 11, with the times keiwv measured (6:09 on the clean run). Each segment here has a little more to say. Controls are verified in `demo-run-sheet.md`, cut C. Spanish version: `script-8min-es.md`.

**Thesis:** on Mars, the question isn't where you can go. It's where you can go and still get home.

| Time | Beat | Who | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Opening | P1 | Jezero crater, Mars. An astronaut stands at Cheyava Falls, the rock where Perseverance found a potential biosignature. The habitat is 913 metres away. Getting back costs her 18 minutes and 54 grams of oxygen. And a dust storm is coming. |
| 0:20 | Thesis | P1 | On Mars, the question isn't where you can go. It's where you can go and still get home. We are Los Frijolitos Espaciales Refritos Nueva Generación, from Ensenada. This is Marswalk. |
| 0:35 | Demo 1 · Start | P2 | Two missions, the short First sortie and the full EVA to Cheyava Falls. We open the mission table. |
| 0:48 | Demo 2 · Site and layers | P2 | We're at Neretva Vallis, an ancient Jezero river. The map is the HiRISE image at 25 centimetres. Each layer says what it decides. Terrain: the slope, and where the route turns. It never crosses red, over 20 degrees. Rocks: what walking costs. Outcrop: where the crew stands. Radio: where contact with the habitat drops. Exposure: how much sky is over you if radiation arrives. And the white line: where Perseverance drove. |
| 1:43 | Demo 3 · Needs and route | P2 | The crew says what it needs and the route follows. If we ask to Sample Cheyava Falls, the route grows to Bright Angel and Cheyava Falls. This is Perseverance's photo of the rock. If the crew is cautious, slope costs more and the reserve grows. Each leg says why it goes where it goes. Three profiles, the fastest, the gentlest, and the one that keeps radio. We go back to First sortie. |
| 2:43 | Demo 4 · Go or no go | P2 | Hazards, when each one arrives in this mission. Suit, MEDA's forecast for the walk, and the decision, go. We Begin EVA. |
| 3:06 | Demo 5 · Walk | P2 | The visor. On the left, the margin, the oxygen I have left after walking home and keeping the 30 minute reserve. Below, position. No GPS, so the suit matches the terrain to HiRISE, with its error circle. There's pale rock off the route. I mark it, and the system tells me what the detour costs in time and oxygen, and whether I can afford it. I can. I add it. (Walk.) |
| 4:25 | Demo 6 · Sample | P2 | We're at S1. Photo, sample, stow and seal. The list fills up. |
| 4:43 | Demo 7 · Storm | P2 | Dust storm. The opacity climbs to the 2018 global storm Curiosity measured at Gale. I open the plan, and the route already heads to the habitat. |
| 5:03 | Demo 8 · Radiation and return | P2 | Now the radiation, real doses RAD measured in September 2017, at Gale, not Jezero, and the screen says so. The rule is to shelter early. Second re-plan. We walk back and go in. (Walk.) |
| 6:03 | Demo 9 · Habitat table | P2 | In the cabin, E opens the plan, X ends the EVA. |
| 6:11 | Demo 10 · Debrief | P2 | The debrief compares the approved route with the walked one. The detour to the pale rock was worth it, because we marked a find with margin to spare. And every number says if it is DATA, DERIVED or SIM. |
| 6:46 | Demo 11 · Sources | P2 | And here, every dataset and every model we used. |

# Presentation beats

The talk runs in the presentation mode on `main` (#330): https://martian-map.fernando-haro-c.workers.dev/?present=1.
Its beats live in `src/present/beats.js`; this file follows them. The table below is generated by
`node docs/pitch/tools/sync-present.mjs`: each beat's captions, chips and source line as the judges see them, with the
numbers from `public/present/plan.json`, and the line `script-en.md` says over it. Do not edit the table by hand.

> **Format.** At most 4 minutes, video included, with a 3:40 target (Fernando, 15 November). The recorded app video
> is the `demo` beat and replaces the live demo. One **Right** per beat. The organisers' page quoted in
> `docs/SUBMISSION.md` says 3 minutes at most, and the team confirms the 4 with them.

Keys: **Right**, **Space**, **PageDown** or **Enter** go forward; **Left** or **PageUp** go back; **L** switches
English and Spanish; **Esc** leaves the presentation. If WebGL fails, the page shows "3D view unavailable. Captions
only"; use `out/deck-global.pdf` instead (`demo-run-sheet.md`).

**Which beats.** Seven beats from #356 on `main`: `open` 20 s, `plan` 24 s, `zone` 24 s, `radiation` 16 s, `demo`
73.8 s, `sources` 18 s and `credits` 22 s, 3:18 under autoplay. The eighth, `live`, hands over to the app. Do not
press Right into it.

## The demo beat's scenes

The recording (`public/present/demo.mp4`, Spanish cut `demo-es.mp4`) runs 73.8 s: 0:00 the zone with 1 h, 0:05 the
zone with 30 min, 0:12 the crew's needs re-plan the route, 0:24 out through the airlock, 0:30 a dust storm and EV2's
"storm front", 0:38 the radiation alert with EV2 walking home ahead, 0:58 back through the airlock, 1:04 the review of
the walked track against the plan. The cues in the script's `demo` row follow that order. If the cut changes, change
the cues in both scripts and rerun `sync-present.mjs`.

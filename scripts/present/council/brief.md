# Council brief: Marswalk presentation mode

Each judge gets this brief, the persona line, and the frame folder of the round. No judge sees another judge's answer.

## What you are judging

Marswalk is a team's entry for NASA Space Apps 2026, Mars track: a browser EVA simulator on real HiRISE terrain at
Cheyava Falls, Jezero crater. The team pitches inside the app with a cinematic presentation mode. It starts at Mars from
orbit, falls to Jezero, lands on the 3D terrain, and makes one argument:

> On Mars, the question isn't where you can go. It's where you can go and still get home.

The hero shot is the walkback zone (orange): everywhere the astronaut can reach and still get home within the crew's
planned time, from the app's own zone module. It tightens as the clock runs and drops when the radiation alert comes.
The last beat hands over to the live app.

The creative rules the team set for itself (break one and it is a blocker):

- Bold: one unbroken move from the planet to 1 m ground; real numbers from the app with a DATA, DERIVED or SIM tag;
  one accent colour (orange) only for danger and the zone.
- Banned visuals: nebulae, lens flares, blue holograms, glowing grid floors, particle swirls, a stock or AI astronaut,
  typewriter text, spinning logos.
- Banned words: revolutionary, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, next-generation, immersive, seamless, powerful,
  innovative, "the final frontier", "one small step", "imagine a world where", "introducing", "the future of
  exploration", "AI-powered", stacked rhetorical questions, "...and beyond".
- Captions at most 8 words, no bullet lists on screen, one idea per beat.
- Order of questions: where are we, why there, what is the danger, what Marswalk does about it, how we know the
  numbers are real, can I see it work. The last beat is the live demo.

## What to look at

Frames are in the folder you are given, in time order. Sheets named `beat-*.jpg` are one beat each, read left to right,
top to bottom, at the frame rate printed in the file name. `overview.jpg` is the whole presentation. `global30-*.jpg` is
the 30 s video for the global judges, which must stand on its own with no narration.

The numbers on screen come from `public/present/plan.json`: route 913 m, legs 356 / 255 / 242 / 60 m, 44.8 g of O2 out,
24.6 deg slope beside the bend (no-go over 20 deg), suit limit 8 h, reserve 30 min, sortie plan 30 min (25.5 min
usable), zone 32 ha at the start, 15 ha at T+12 min, 1.4 ha after the alert, RAD peak 26 uGy/h (Curiosity, Gale crater,
September 2017), dust opacity 8.0 (Curiosity, Gale, 2018 storm), terrain against NASA Mars Trek 0.14 m RMS.

## What to send back

Reasons, not scores. Plain text, under 500 words.

1. Blockers: anything that would embarrass the team in front of judges or breaks a rule above. Quote the frame.
2. Majors: things that weaken the pitch a lot.
3. Minors.
4. Which animation teaches nothing and should be cut.
5. Would you open the pitch with this? Yes or no, and why in two sentences.
